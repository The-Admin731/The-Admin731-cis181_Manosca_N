Advanced Performance and Modding

For members who want to push their computers to the absolute limit of performance and custom looks.

Introduction to Safe Overclocking

Overclocking means increasing the clock speed of components like the processor and graphics card above factory settings. This improves performance in heavy games, but requires constant temperature monitoring.

Console and PC Modding

Modding focuses on customizing the computer case or chassis visually. This includes installing custom liquid cooling systems, painting components, and neat cable management.
