Our Mission

The Wright College PC Gaming and Modding Club is a student organization created to bring together all computer hardware, game development, and modding enthusiasts at Wilbur Wright College.

Our goal is to provide a safe and educational space where beginners and experts can share their experiences, learn how to build computers safely, and optimize their systems to the max.

Join the Community!

It does not matter if you have never opened a computer or if you are already an overclocking expert; there is a place for you here.
